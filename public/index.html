Amalyzer

Windowed damage from a Warcraft Logs report — who to buff, per 27-second window.

Warcraft Logs credentials

Create a client at warcraftlogs.com/api/clients (any redirect URL). Credentials are kept in this tab's session storage and never on a server.

Report

Load fights fills the fight list from the report. Each window is a Prescience cast plus the aura's measured lifetime (the fallback 18s is only used when the log is missing the removal), so the damage shown is what happened while that person was buffed. Filtering uses the default ability filter (drops non-scaling trinket damage); leave it alone unless you know why. After analysing you can switch between DPS and raw damage, hide Augmentation Evokers, or compare buffed vs unbuffed targets without re-querying.
